# Differential-equations chapter additions

`continuation.html` is the guided textbook narrative from a local logarithm
to Fuchs. The publication pass inserts it after local uniqueness, before the
monodromy reference section and ordinary forcing. Its interactive example
appears beside the narrative on desktop and after its first result on mobile.
The right rail retains a chapter contents menu.

The chapter is the canonical destination. The old `road-to-fuchs.html` URL
redirects here with its section fragment preserved. Theorem shortcuts such
as `fuchs.html` contain setup and statement first, followed by supporting
explanation and links back to this textbook development.
