01 / Assumptions

A mathematical model you can check

Use an electron in the static Coulomb field of a fixed point nucleus. Measure distances in reduced Compton units and energies in electron rest energy units. The stationary equation is

\[\left[-i\sum_{j=1}^{3}\mathsf A_j\partial_j+\mathsf B-\frac{\alpha}{r}I_4\right]\psi=\varepsilon\psi,\qquad r=\sqrt{x_1^2+x_2^2+x_3^2}.\]

Here \(\mathsf A_j=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sigma_j\\\sigma_j&0\end{pmatrix}\), \(\mathsf B=\operatorname{diag}(I_2,-I_2)\), and \(\sigma_j\) are the Pauli matrices. The dimensionless energy is \(\varepsilon=E/(m_ec^2)\), including rest energy. The only numerical parameter in the equation is the coupling \(\alpha\).

One convenient common range
\[0<\alpha\leq\frac1{16},\qquad u:=\alpha^2.\]

The input is a valid represented value of \(u\); it may be irrational. No decimal value of the physical fine structure constant is inserted. The formal results below also state their wider individual coupling ranges.

This fixed nucleus model contains no proton mass parameter. Nuclear recoil, finite nuclear size, radiative corrections and hyperfine interactions belong to different models. These inequalities bound the mathematical remainders within the stated equation; they are not bounds on those additional physical effects.

02 / Exact consequences

From radial solutions to closed-graph eigenpairs

Write \(k=|\kappa|\geq1\) for the angular channel label and \(N\geq0\) for the radial polynomial degree. The constructed positive energy is

\[\gamma_k=\sqrt{k^2-u},\qquad \varepsilon_{k,N}(u)=\frac{N+\gamma_k}{\sqrt{(N+\gamma_k)^2+u}}.\]

These are computations with proved square root and reciprocal laws. The construction supplies regular radial solutions and proves their differential equations and integrability. Conversely, the regular radial graph theorem classifies its positive gap eigenstates by the admissible degrees. On the negative signed channel \(N=0\) is allowed; on the positive signed channel \(N\geq1\).

The constructed Cartesian states also satisfy a completed graph theorem. Let \(\mathcal G_\alpha\) be the norm closure of the original finite compact-test Dirac pairs. For the admissible channels above and \(0<u\leq1/4\), the construction proves

\[\bigl(\Psi_{k,N},\,\varepsilon_{k,N}\Psi_{k,N}\bigr)\in\mathcal G_\alpha,\qquad \|\Psi_{k,N}\|^2=1.\]

The independently differentiated action converges alongside the state. The proof identifies the physical annular states with the original finite-grid limits and removes both cutoffs; no value at the singular origin is needed. This establishes actual nonzero eigenpairs in the closed relation. It does not yet prove that this relation is a self-adjoint operator or that these states exhaust its spectrum.

The hypotheses of the regular radial classification

Consider a nonzero differentiable radial pair \((G,F)\) solving the radial Dirac equations with \(0<\varepsilon<1\). Each component has the regular origin branch \(r^{\gamma_k}R(r)\), where \(R\) extends continuously to a neighborhood of the origin. Both the source pair and its Dirac action have justified square integrals. In this stated graph domain, the energy must equal \(\varepsilon_{k,N}\) for an admissible degree; conversely, each admissible degree has a constructed eigenstate.

The theorem concerns this regular separated domain. It does not assert that every vector in an unrestricted completed Cartesian operator domain has already been reduced to this form.

Each gap is the upper energy minus the lower energy. Both are strictly positive for nonzero coupling. The same electron rest energy factor multiplies every dimensional gap, so ratios of these gaps require no mass measurement.

An exact formula that avoids subtracting nearly equal energies

For \(k\geq1\), set

\[d_k=(1+\gamma_k)^2+u,\quad H_k=\frac{\gamma_{k+1}}{k+1},\quad L_k=\frac{1+\gamma_k}{\sqrt{d_k}},\]
\[D_k=(\gamma_k+k)(k+1)^2d_k(H_k+L_k).\]

The proved identity is

\[\Delta_k:=\varepsilon_{k+1,0}-\varepsilon_{k,1}=\frac{2u^2}{D_k},\qquad D_k>0.\]

Thus \(\Delta_{2p}=\Delta_1\), \(\Delta_{3d}=\Delta_2\), and their exact ratio is \(D_1/D_2\). This expression exposes the common fourth power of the coupling before numerical evaluation.

03 / Quantitative theorems

Keep the error in the answer

Theorem 1Inverse square binding law

For the constructed circular state with principal label \(n\geq1\), let \(\varepsilon_n=\sqrt{1-u/n^2}\) and \(b_n=1-\varepsilon_n\). For \(0\leq u<1\),

\[0\leq b_n-\frac{u}{2n^2}=\frac{b_n^2}{2}\leq\frac{u^2}{2n^4}.\]

The familiar inverse square law comes with an exact identity for its error. This statement concerns the circular family, not every fine structure level at a fixed principal label.

Theorem 2Two fine structure doublets
\[\begin{aligned}\left|\Delta_{2p}-\frac{u^2}{32}\right|&\leq u^3 &&\left(0<u\leq\frac14\right),\\[6pt]\left|\Delta_{3d}-\frac{u^2}{324}\right|&\leq\frac{u^3}{64} &&\left(0<u\leq\frac1{16}\right).\end{aligned}\]

These bounds apply to gaps between the constructed normalized eigenpairs in the original closed Dirac relation. They are exact inequalities at finite coupling, rather than a discarded higher order term.

Theorem 3A ratio with no energy scale
\[\boxed{\left|\frac{\Delta_{3d}}{\Delta_{2p}}-\frac8{81}\right|\leq8u\qquad\left(0<u\leq\frac1{128}\right).}\]

The denominator is proved positive. The exact ratio has a representation independent interval evaluator: for every positive rational tolerance, it produces a rational enclosure of at most that width. The limiting ratio is \(8/81\), and the displayed bound controls its finite coupling error.

A purely symbolic specialization

What does the common cap buy?

Under \(0<\alpha\leq1/16\), divide each absolute gap error by its leading term. The displayed bounds immediately give

\[\frac{|\Delta_{2p}-\alpha^4/32|}{\alpha^4/32}\leq\frac18,\qquad \frac{|\Delta_{3d}-\alpha^4/324|}{\alpha^4/324}\leq\frac{81}{4096}.\]

The ratio differs from \(8/81\) by at most \(1/32\). These are algebraic consequences of the displayed theorems. Tighter symbolic caps make all three errors smaller; no measured value is needed.

04 / Why this is a foundation example

An exact value is a computation with a proof

A represented real is supplied by rational interval computations with validity and shrinking width proofs. Square roots, reciprocals, radial functions, integrals and energy ratios are built on those computations. Equality means equality of the values represented, expressed in Lean by RealRaw.Equiv.

  1. Construct the parameters.

    Positive roots and reciprocals produce the energies. Dispersion and quantization identities are proved; uniqueness identifies the positive solution.

  2. Construct the states.

    Polynomial radial solutions, regular origin behavior, normalization and Cartesian differential equations supply the actual states. Removing the cutoffs in both state and action norm places these eigenpairs in the original closed relation.

  3. Prove the comparison.

    Rationalized gap identities avoid cancellation. Explicit inequalities bound the energy and ratio remainders, and equivalent input representations give equivalent outputs.

The native mathematics uses this project's real and complex computations, without importing Mathlib's real or complex number systems. The source links below expose the exact hypotheses and constructions.

Scope / Read alongside the theorems

What is established, and what remains

Established in the stated setting

Exact energy computations, regular radial classification, actual Cartesian differential equations, normalized nonzero eigenpairs in the original compact-test closed relation, and the splitting and ratio bounds above.

The remaining operator bridge

Self-adjointness, single-valuedness of the closed relation, and exhaustion of its unrestricted Cartesian spectrum remain open. The physical annular states are now identified with the original grid limits, and both cutoffs have been removed in the original closed relation.

This is a showpiece for exact constructive mathematics within an explicit model. The differential equation, channel and boundary hypotheses belong to its contract. The full physical hydrogen spectrum is a larger claim.
