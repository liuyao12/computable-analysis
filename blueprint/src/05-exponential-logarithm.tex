\chapter{Exponential and Logarithm}\label{ch:exponential-logarithm}

Exponential, logarithm, and rotation are explicit rational computations.
Their algebraic and differential laws are overlap theorems between finite
prefixes with certified tails; no completed line or complex plane is assumed.

\section{Exponential}

\begin{verbatim}
input x -> choose N from tail budget -> sum x^k/k! -> enclose remainder
\end{verbatim}

The evaluator is a finite Taylor prefix plus a factorial majorant.  The
tail proof is potential infinity: every later finite prefix remains in the
same rational enclosure once the budget is chosen.

\section{Rotation and complex variables}

\section{Logarithm}\label{sec:logarithm}

On a positive rational chart, logarithm has two useful raw implementations:
a reciprocal integral and an alternating/harmonic series.  The complete

\section{A product FTC \(\pi\) bridge}

The first non-polynomial product calculation is concrete:
\[
 F(x)=x\arctan(x),\qquad F'(x)=\arctan(x)+\frac{x}{1+x^2}.
\]
Two finite derivative-bound stages identify its integral on \([0,1]\) with
\(\arctan(1)\).  A finite mesh reindexing then gives
\[
 4\,\operatorname{arctanTriangle}+2\int_1^2\frac{dt}{t}
 =4\,\operatorname{arctan}(1).
\]
It is an instance, not a general integration-by-parts axiom.
