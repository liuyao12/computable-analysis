\begin{verbatim}
prefix -> finite identity -> tail bound -> nested enclosure
\end{verbatim}

\section{Pi and power series}

Crude rectangles, binomial expansions, termwise integration, and Machin-type
identities are different routes through the same space of computations. This
is a methodological observation, not a claim about Newton's motives.
\begin{verbatim}
choose route -> compute prefix -> compare enclosures -> refine
\end{verbatim}
