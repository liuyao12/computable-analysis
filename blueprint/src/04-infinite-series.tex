\chapter{Power Series and Finite Fourier Algebra}\label{ch:infinite-series}

Potential infinity is a program that extends a finite prefix together with a
rational tail bound.  No completed sum is created behind the notation.

\begin{verbatim}
prefix N -> rational tail bound -> interval for every later prefix
         -> nested, shrinking realRaw
\end{verbatim}

\section{Series certificates}

A series evaluator supplies finite partial sums and a majorant for the
unseen terms.  Alternating series use the next term; positive geometric tails
use a finite ratio estimate; factorial tails use a finite comparison.

\begin{verbatim}
choose N from requested budget
compute sum(k = 0..N)
attach [lower tail, upper tail]
\end{verbatim}

\section{Arctangent and \(\pi\)}

The alternating arctangent prefix is the first circle computation:
\[
 \arctan(x)=x-x^3/3+x^5/5-\cdots\qquad (0\leq x\leq1).
\]
At \(x=1\), four times its certified raw is compared with the rational-circle
quarter turn.  The value of \(\pi\) is the resulting chain of raw
equivalences, not a primitive real constant.

\begin{verbatim}
alternating arctan prefix + next-term box
      -> arctan.geom(1) -> fourfold circle-area presentation
\end{verbatim}

\section{Power series and calculus}

Finite coefficient algebra gives derivative and integral identities for a
prefix.  A tail certificate then transports the identity to the corresponding
raw algorithms.  This is the route used by exponential, rotation, and the
special-function prefixes in later chapters.

\begin{verbatim}
coefficient shift -> finite derivative/integral identity
                  -> tail budget -> effective FTC certificate

\section{Finite Fourier algebra}

Fourier begins with finite complex transforms: roots of unity, finite sums,
inverse transform, and convolution are exact rational-complex calculations.
The project uses complex variables from the start; real sine and cosine are
coordinates of a rotation computation.

\begin{verbatim}
finite samples -> complex transform -> finite coefficient identity
               -> explicit truncation/tail certificate (when available)
\end{verbatim}

overlap with any alternative implementation.
