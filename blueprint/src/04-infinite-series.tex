\chapter{Infinite Series}\label{ch:infinite-series}

An infinite object is a sequence of finite computations with a certified tail.

\section{Finite stages}

Fourier, Parseval, power sums, geometric sums, and prime-reciprocal prefixes
are finite certificates, not completed limits.
\begin{verbatim}
forward := finite mode sums
inverse := recombine coordinates / sample count
tail   := explicit rational bound
\end{verbatim}
\label{sec:series-benchmark-foundations}\label{thm:finite-fourier-orthogonality}
\label{thm:finite-fourier-interface}
\emph{Fourier interface.} A finite rational transform reconstructs its sample
vector and satisfies a finite Parseval identity; no infinite series is hidden.
\label{thm:finite-prime-reciprocal-extension}\label{thm:arithmetic-series-sum}
\label{thm:finite-power-sums}\label{thm:geometric-series-sum}
\label{thm:finite-geometric-series-interface}
\emph{Geometric interface.} Compute the finite prefix recursively and carry
an explicit tail bound; the completed sum is a later identification.
\label{thm:reciprocal-triangular-sum}\label{thm:harmonic-dyadic-lower-bound}
\label{thm:finite-reciprocal-triangular-interface}
\label{thm:finite-power-sum-interface}
\emph{Finite sum interfaces.} Reciprocal triangular terms telescope with an
explicit tail, while power sums have checked polynomial forms at finite stage.
\label{thm:finite-prime-reciprocal-interface}
The prime-reciprocal construction extends any finite certified prime list and
strictly raises its rational accumulator; divergence is the potential-infinity
interpretation of this extension.

\section{Basel and factorial growth}

Finite ratio and comparison certificates precede constructive moduli for the
full limits.
