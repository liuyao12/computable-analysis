\section{Taylor and finite L'Hôpital}

Factor the known polynomial part, bound the remainder, refine.
Finite Taylor stages integrate term by term through an exact endpoint
recurrence; the omitted tail is handled separately by a rational budget.
\label{thm:finite-taylor-ftc-prefix}
\emph{Taylor interface.} Integrate a finite coefficient prefix exactly; treat every omitted remainder as a separate rational error budget.
\begin{verbatim}
prefix := integratedTaylorPrefix coefficients terms
increment := prefix(b) - prefix(a)
fold := sum_{k < terms} c[k] * (b^(k+1)-a^(k+1))/(k+1)
\end{verbatim}
