\section{Secants before completeness}

The historical core is the mean-value relation, treated here as a finite
certificate rather than as a consequence of completed real numbers.
\begin{verbatim}
endpoint increment -> derivative bounds -> rational witness interval
\end{verbatim}
For monotone derivatives or convex functions, the witness is especially
direct: the endpoint increment lies between the two derivative bounds.

\section{FTC reconstruction}

Rectangle sums and derivative bounds are two descriptions of the same finite
increment. Refine until their intervals overlap; then package the result as
the computable FTC.
\begin{verbatim}
partition -> lower/upper sums -> derivative comparison -> overlap
\end{verbatim}
