A root is a rational box whose polynomial image can meet zero.

      -> discard boxes missing zero

\end{verbatim}

included.  Global termination requires an explicit modulus.

\section{Boundary}

There is no general radical formula in degree five.  Effective isolation is
the computable substitute.  The finite sign-count interface does not claim
the classical “number of positive roots is at most the variations” theorem;
one-/zero-variation root certificates are supplied separately.
