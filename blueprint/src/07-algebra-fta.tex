\section{Boundary}

Degree five has no general radical formula.  The supplied-root quintic
certificate peels five exact rational-complex factors and records the
quotient after each step.  This is the computable Abel--Ruffini boundary, not
a general quintic solver.  Global termination, algebraic closure, and a
radical presentation are separate bridges.
