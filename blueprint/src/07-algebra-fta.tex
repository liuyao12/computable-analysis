\section{Boundary}

Degree five has no general radical formula.  The finite certificate is the
computable core of the cubic, quartic, factor/remainder, Descartes, and
Abel--Ruffini entries in the theorem list.  Global termination, algebraic
closure, and a radical presentation are separate bridges.
