\chapter{Integrals}

Each integral begins as a rational-box algorithm.  Its separate validity
certificate is what makes the output a \code{RealRaw}; there is no ambient
completed-real integral or table of antiderivatives.

\begin{theorem}[Finite geometric integration by parts]
\label{thm:finite-geometric-integration-by-parts}
