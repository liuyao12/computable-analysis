\section{Archimedes and rational circles}

The point is not an optimal polygon. It is a family of crude subdivisions whose
lower and upper bounds are related and eventually overlap.
\begin{verbatim}
subdivide -> certify each cell -> sum bounds -> refine
\end{verbatim}
