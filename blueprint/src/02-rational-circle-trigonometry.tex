The essential\npseudocode is:\n\begin{verbatim}\nangle(x,n) := binary_search(area, x, n)\npoint(x,n) := circle_point(angle(x,n))\nsin, cos := coordinate intervals of point\n\end{verbatim}\n\n\begin{theorem}[Sector density and pi]\label{thm:sector-area-density}\nSector area, inverse arctangent, and circle area agree at every requested\nprecision; the endpoint bridge yields the project's (pi).\n\end{theorem}\n\begin{theorem}[Rational circle functions]\label{thm:rational-circle-powers}\nThe point algorithm supplies addition, reflection, Pythagorean, and de\nMoivre identities as finite interval certificates.\n\end{theorem}\n\label{thm:geometric-pi-validity}\label{thm:geometric-arctan-one-area-pi}\n\label{thm:rational-circle-basic-identities}\label{thm:dyadic-trig-samples}\n\label{thm:geometric-addition-formulas}\label{thm:rational-circle-group-law}\n\n\section{Boundary of the chapter}\n\nThe rational parameter line is a computational chart, not a claim that every\nclassical real function has a rational-name representation.
