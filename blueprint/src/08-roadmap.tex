\chapter{Formalization Roadmap}

\section{Admission rule}

Admit a result when it ends in a rational certificate, a shrinking `realRaw`
algorithm, a valid `real`, or an overlap statement.  Reuse routine finite
combinatorics unless it is a stage of an infinite process or an error bound.

\section{Order of work}

\begin{enumerate}
\item interval arithmetic and equality of names;
\item circle, rectangles, and trigonometry;
\item tails, termwise identities, and pi;
\item convex secants, MVT, and FTC;
\item algebraic isolation and linear ODEs.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Style rule}

The blueprint gives the idea, pseudocode, and GIF.  Lean carries hypotheses,
moduli, names, and proofs.  Only `realRaw` and `real` are named here to
distinguish the project's foundation from standard real numbers.
